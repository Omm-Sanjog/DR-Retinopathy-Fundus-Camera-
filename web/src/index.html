Using a phone lens kit

  1. Kit: a 20 D (or 28 D) aspheric condensing lens, ideally on a phone clip/adapter that keeps it in line with the main camera. Commercial smartphone fundus adapters also work.
  2. Room: dim the lights. A dilated pupil gives far better images (only as advised by a clinician).
  3. Light: tap Torch in the live camera — the phone LED next to the lens lights the retina.
  4. Align: hold the lens about 5 cm from the eye. Move the phone until the orange-red reflex fills the lens.
  5. Focus: slide the phone slowly back and forth until the vessels are sharp. Use zoom (2×) if the retina looks small.
  6. Capture: when Retina, Focus and Light turn green, FundusScan captures the sharpest frame automatically.

Safety: keep each attempt short (under about a minute of torch light per eye) and stop if the person is uncomfortable. This is a research prototype, not a certified medical device — every result must be confirmed by an eye-care professional.
